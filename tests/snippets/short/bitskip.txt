>>> Issue 0140: the BIT skip trick. Falling into a one-byte data line holding
$2c (bit abs) or $24 (bit zp) runs the bit, which skips the instruction hidden
in its operand: no warning, and the flow goes on after the operand. An
lda #$00 / beq is always taken: it does not fall through into the table after
it, so that table gives no warning either, and neither do clc / bcc and
lda #$80 / bmi. <<<

*=$c000

entry:
		jsr set_one
		jsr set_zero
		jsr set_carry
		jsr clear_carry
		lda #$00
		beq done
table:
		.byte	$01, $02
done:
		clc 
		bcc done2
table2:
		.byte	$03
done2:
		lda #$80
		bmi done3
table3:
		.byte	$04
done3:
		rts 

set_one:
		lda #$01
skip_abs:
		.byte	$2c
set_zero:
		lda #$00
		sta $fb
		rts 

set_carry:
		sec 
skip_zp:
		.byte	$24
clear_carry:
		clc 
		rts 
===
